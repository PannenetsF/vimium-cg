This page lists all available commands in Vimium C (up to v1.89.1).
The list of available options can be seen in `CmdOptions` (for most frontend commands) of
  [types/messages.d.ts#CmdOptions](https://github.com/gdh1995/vimium-c/blob/master/typings/messages.d.ts#:~:text=interface%20CmdOptions)
  and `BgCmdOptions` (for other commands) of
  [background/typed_commands.d.ts](https://github.com/gdh1995/vimium-c/blob/master/background/typed_commands.d.ts#:~:text=interface%20BgCmdOptions),
  and the mapping from command names to `kFgCmd` and `kBgCmd` values is in
  [background/typed_commands.d.ts#CmdNameIds](https://github.com/gdh1995/vimium-c/blob/master/background/typed_commands.d.ts#:~:text=interface%20CmdNameIds).

The short descriptions of all commands are listed in:
* English: https://github.com/gdh1995/vimium-c/blob/master/i18n/en/help_dialog.json#:~:text=LinkHints.activate
* 中文（Chinese）： https://github.com/gdh1995/vimium-c/blob/master/i18n/zh/help_dialog.json#:~:text=LinkHints.activate
* French： https://github.com/gdh1995/vimium-c/blob/master/i18n/fr/help_dialog.json#:~:text=LinkHints.activate
